# Terminology

- **AHMP** — Agentic HyperMarketing Protocol.
- **B2A Marketing** — Business-to-Agent marketing: a business proposes a machine-readable offer to an agent acting for a consumer instead of directly buying human attention.
- **Consumer Agent** — agent whose authority comes from the consumer and whose primary objective is consumer utility.
- **Merchant Marketing Agent** — agent acting for a seller and proposing offers or counteroffers.
- **Marketing Broker** — optional neutral coordination service. It MUST NOT override consumer constraints.
- **Demand Contract** — consumer-governed machine-readable constraints describing explicit demand, categories, budgets, timing, channels, modalities, discovery limits and privacy preferences.
- **Offer Proposal** — structured commercial proposal evaluated before presentation to the human.
- **Eligibility** — deterministic constraint evaluation that happens before ranking or bidding.
- **Discovery** — controlled exploration of complementary products whose utility is uncertain.
- **Baseline** — high-confidence action based on known recurring or previously preferred products.
- **Recovery** — optional decision after rejected discovery. A discount is an eligible action, never an automatic reward for rejection.
- **No Action** — first-class decision to avoid an interruption.
- **Interruption Budget** — limit on how often the system may present marketing to a consumer.
- **Complement Graph** — relation graph describing which products or services complement another product or state.
- **Ownership State** — known purchased/owned products used for suppression, lifecycle and complement reasoning.
