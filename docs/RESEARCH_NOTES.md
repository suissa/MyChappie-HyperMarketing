# Research Notes

AHMP combines established concepts rather than claiming that each component is novel.

## Related concepts

- contextual multi-armed bandits;
- conservative/safe exploration;
- next-best-action / next-best-offer;
- complementary-product recommendation;
- cascading/sequential recommendations;
- preference centers and channel consent;
- agentic commerce and machine-readable business capabilities.

## Distinctive composition proposed here

The architectural contribution is the combination of:

```text
explicit consumer demand
+ hard communication constraints
+ purchase/ownership state
+ lifecycle-aware suppression/replenishment
+ complement graph
+ conservative contextual discovery
+ baseline recovery
+ no_action
+ B2A machine-readable offers
+ selective merchant feedback
```

with a protocol boundary that makes **consumer eligibility precede advertiser competition**.

## Evaluation questions

A research implementation should compare AHMP-style decisioning against a conventional ranking baseline on:

- redundant-impression reduction;
- long-term conversion value;
- number of interruptions per purchase;
- consumer opt-out/unsubscribe rate;
- useful-offer rate;
- discovery regret;
- merchant margin after discounts;
- privacy/disclosure surface.

## Important experimental warning

Offline evaluation can underestimate exploration value when logs were generated by a highly exploitative behavior policy. Production experimentation should preserve propensity/policy metadata and use appropriate causal/off-policy evaluation methods.
